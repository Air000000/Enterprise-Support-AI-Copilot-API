# Offline v5.3 question-scoped coverage candidate

Status: **prompt candidate only; zero provider calls, no production integration**.

The [natural diagnosis](natural_v5_2_1_result.md) found two new sufficient-case
proxy refusals driven by extra conditional material requirements, and a shared
insufficient admission based on topic overlap without incident applicability proof.
These interpretations use already-observed AI-proxy development material, not
independent expert gold. All old labels, prompts, contracts, raw journals and
scores stay immutable; they are not rescored or retrospectively repaired.

The new policy derives requirements from the question and genuinely necessary
dependencies. Optional platforms or different symptoms in retrieved distractors
do not automatically become obligations. Real same-claim/same-scope conflicts,
explicit version/time constraints and necessary conditions still block admission.
For incident claims, shared names alone are insufficient: distinguish visible
mechanism/prerequisite matches from unknown facts. General possible-cause/check
requests and incidents with established relevant prerequisites may be supported;
there is no universal incident refusal or exact-exception-string requirement.
A causal attribution in a question is a claim to check, not independent confirmation.

No new response fields, keyword rules, source filtering, added model stage or
post-processing repair. Full ordered numbered user payload and the exact v5.2
validator callable are reused. The change is instructions, not a semantic oracle:
valid references with false SUPPORTED judgments can still pass. If the model
adds an unnecessary CONDITIONAL requirement, the gate still refuses; it does not
delete that requirement after generation.

Handwritten fictional traces cover optional-platform distractors, different-symptom
fixes, missing versus established incident prerequisites, general diagnostics and
real conflicts. Tests verify payload/wiring and unchanged mechanical gates, not
that a model follows the new policy or that refusal quality/cost improved. No real
question/source text or per-case target rows are published. Future execution needs
a distinct scoped protocol/checkpoint/authority; observed natural cases and derived
fixtures are development regressions, never an unseen acceptance set.
